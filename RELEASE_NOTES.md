## DJSIM by Raygun: macOS

**Which file do I download?**
- Apple Silicon Mac (M1/M2/M3/M4): **`DJSIM-1.0.0-arm64.dmg`**
- Intel Mac: **`DJSIM-1.0.0-x64.dmg`**

Open the DMG and drag DJSIM to Applications. This build isn't notarized, so on first launch right-click the app → **Open** → **Open** (or run `xattr -cr /Applications/DJSIM.app`).

### Highlights
- A 3D crowd of up to 2,500 people where everyone has a name, archetype, backstory, goal and live story log. Click anyone to follow them.
- 30 procedural dance moves driven by genre, section, taste and stamina; staggered drop reactions, mosh pits, dance circles, shoulder rides and crowd surfing.
- Recognises 78 dance anthems by title/artist, with a recognition wave through the crowd.
- 21 worlds, 15 LED visualizers, 9 laser modes, 10 moving-head patterns, 16 palettes, 44 presets, 8 camera modes plus a cinematic auto-director and Auto-VJ.
- Audio-thread beat tracking (BPM, beat phase, bars, phrases), section and drop detection, genre estimation.
- Sources: built-in Demo DJ, your own files with auto-mixing, live input from a mixer or interface, experimental system audio.

Press **H** in the app for all keyboard shortcuts.
